# EDGE-001-registry-derived-dns

> **Naming**: file lives at `<repo>/specs/EDGE-001-registry-derived-dns/proposal.md`. `EDGE-001-registry-derived-dns` is `AREA-NNN-slug` (e.g. `TOOL-001-secret-drift`).

## Why

<!-- from issue #3495: DNS: derive staging split-DNS zones from the product registry and reconcile on merge -->

Onboarding imagesensortool (#3479) showed that self-service ends at the cluster boundary. Its staging zone had to be hand-written into `networking.staging_zones`, although its contract snapshot already names `hostnames.staging`. Its ACME zone was never probed, because `acme-token-scope` reads only `edge.traefik.acme_domains` (#3482). Today a product with no hand-written zone renders, deploys and resolves nowhere, and a token that cannot issue for its zone passes the check. Every future product, resume first (#2366), pays the same hand steps.

## What

One derived edge manifest, `infra/config/products/edge.json`, is generated by `toolkit sync edge-dns` and checked by `make validate-sync` like `platform.json`. In this PR it carries `staging_zones`: the platform's declared zones (still `networking.staging_zones` in `common.yaml`), followed in registry order by the staging hostname of each registered product with a staging lane, minus hosts already under a declared zone, deduplicated with the first position kept.

**Trust anchor (owner ruling 2026-11-13).** A product hostname counts only if its apex, the longest suffix that is a key of `infra/terraform/dns/zones.json`, is a Cloudflare zone the platform declares. A contract can choose a name inside a granted zone and can never grant one. D8's refusal therefore names `zones.json` instead of `networking.staging_zones` (amends IDP-053 D8/AC13 and ADR-067 line 91).

Consumers read the manifest, never the hand list:
- the `coredns` role (Corefile and `pihole-forwarding.conf`), via `lookup('file') | from_json`, the same precedent as `services.json`;
- `deploy-vps.yml` (Headscale `split`);
- the pod-side hairpin (`k8s_render.render_staging_zones` through `cli/infra.py`);
- the D8 check, through one toolkit accessor.

The product-image receiver regenerates the manifest in the same bot PR, so a new staging hostname arrives with its zone.

`toolkit secrets acme-token-scope` probes the `acme_domains` mains plus the apex of every registered product hostname, per env (#3482).

## Out of scope

- #3489, prod public records derived from the registry: second PR in this spec (owner ruling: separate PR), including web's `mlorente.dev` apex record and the `proxied` default.
- Reconciling Headscale, CoreDNS and Cloudflare on merge (slice B). It needs ADR-073 accepted with D2/D13 ruled, or an edge ADR.
- `INFRA_FORGE_SOURCES` and anything employer-scoped.

## Risks / open questions

- Zero diff on the live edge depends on order and dedup: the rendered Corefile, Pi-hole forwarding, Headscale split and hairpin must be byte-identical before and after. A characterization test pins them first.
- The `CHECK=1` baseline holds only after the other session's VPS and DNS applies for imagesensortool have landed; confirm before the owner runs them.
- Concurrent edits to `product_guards.py`: the resume session's RES-HOST-001 slice F amends D8. That lands first, then master is merged here.
- Effective-list readers that bypass the accessor would test the wrong list. Every test that read `networking.staging_zones` moves to the accessor.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `edge.json` is generated from the registry, the snapshots, `zones.json` and the declared platform zones. `make validate-sync ENV=staging` and `ENV=prod` exit 0 with `staging.imagesensortool.com` removed from `common.yaml`, and fail when the manifest is edited by hand.
- [ ] AC2: Zero diff. The rendered Corefile and `pihole-forwarding.conf` are byte-identical to master's for today's zones, the Headscale split list is identical item for item, and the hairpin blocks parse to the same YAML data (their comments may differ), and the owner's `make deploy TARGET=vps ENV=prod CHECK=1` and `TARGET=dns ENV=staging CHECK=1` report `changed=0`.
- [ ] AC3: A fixture product with a staging hostname on a new declared apex yields its zone and its probe target; removing it removes both. A hostname whose apex is not in `zones.json` is refused at render and at derivation, naming `zones.json`.
- [ ] AC4: `acme-token-scope` probes the apex of every registered product hostname per env. A test fails when one is not probed. The owner's `make acme-token-scope` lists `imagesensortool.com` with records 200 and settings 403.
- [ ] AC5: The receiver regenerates and commits `edge.json` with the snapshot.
- [ ] AC6: A mutation (`make mutate`) deleting a derivation line turns a test red. `make test` passes in full.
